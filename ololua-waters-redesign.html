How do I request a new piped water connection?

Contact Ololua with your name, location and preferred contact number. The team can confirm service availability, requirements and the next steps.

Can I request a bowser delivery?

Yes, you can enquire about bulk water delivery. Share your location, the quantity you need and when you would like delivery so the team can confirm availability and pricing.

How can I check my water bill?

Use the customer portal when your account access is active, or contact Ololua customer support for assistance with your account.

How do I report a supply or billing issue?

Contact the customer support team and provide your account details, location and a short description of the issue. Avoid sharing passwords or payment PINs.
